About Us

CDLUXHOME — C stands for China, D for our Spanish architects and designers. We are a Chinese furniture exporter offering full-home customization. Collaborating with Spanish designers, we create furniture tailored to European living habits, spatial standards and aesthetics. Combining European contemporary design with mature Chinese manufacturing, we supply premium indoor & outdoor furniture for villas, hotels and furniture partners across Europe. We support ODM, OEM and bespoke custom projects with reliable quality and flexible solutions.
